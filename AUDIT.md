# AUDIT.md — Forensic audit of shivamdengla.in

**Date:** 2026-07-05 · **Auditor:** design/engineering rebuild pass
**Method:** full source read (every component in `src/`), live dev-server walkthrough, full-page captures at 320 / 375 / 768 / 1440 px, asset weighing, DOM inspection.
**Stack detected (not assumed):** Vite 6 + React 18 SPA, Tailwind CSS v4 (`@theme` in `src/index.css`), Framer Motion 11, three.js + GLTFLoader for a 4.8 MB `avatar.glb`, Google-Fonts-hosted Hanken Grotesk, 104 MB of deck PDFs in `public/decks/`, covers generated by `scripts/generate-covers.mjs`. 100 % client-rendered, single route.

**Priority key:** P0 = high impact on the "land a product role" goal, low-to-medium effort. P1 = high impact, higher effort or medium impact, low effort. P2 = polish.

---

## 1. Overall structure & information architecture

**What it is.** One scroll: Hero → StatsStrip → BehindTheDecks (3 cover thumbnails) → About (char-reveal paragraph) → WhatIDo (inverted white list) → Decks (10 sticky-stacked cards) → Writing (one external button) → Contact. `Navbar.tsx` and `Marquee.tsx` exist in the repo but are never imported — dead code and a sign the IA drifted.

**What's wrong.** The scroll order buries the strongest evidence. A hiring manager's decision path is *"who is this → can they think → show me → how do I reach them."* Here, the thinking-proof (the decks) arrives fifth, after a stats bar of retired claims and an about paragraph, and when it arrives it's ten near-identical cards with no thesis, no stat, no narrative — a filmstrip, not a case study. "Behind the Decks" (section 3) shows three covers *before* the visitor knows what a deck is, and the covers are clipped (see §9).

**Benchmark.** Stripe and Mercury case-study pages sequence cover → thesis → killer stat → narrative → outcome. Emil Kowalski's site puts the work index within one scroll of the headline. Nothing here does either.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Re-sequence: hero (POV + open-to-roles signal) → teardown library (the spine) → range/roles → about → writing/subscribe → contact.
- **P0** Give each deck a real case-study surface (own URL, thesis, key stat, narrative) instead of a card with a "Read Deck" PDF link.
- **P1** Delete dead components (`Navbar.tsx`, `Marquee.tsx`) or wire them in deliberately; dead code hides which nav is canonical.

## 2. User journey & conversion

**Trace.** Land → giant "Deal Closer" + T-pose avatar → stats with retired numbers → three clipped covers → a paragraph that renders at 12 % opacity until you scroll it (§10) → a white list → ten PDF links → one Substack button → email. Leaks:
1. Nothing above the fold says **what he wants** (a product role) or **what you should do** (read a teardown / email him). "Get in touch" is the only CTA and it's the *last* step of the journey offered *first*.
2. The proof-of-thinking artifacts open as raw multi-MB PDFs in a new tab (`Decks.tsx` `href={deck.file}` — `MOKOBARA CASE STUDY.pdf` is **19 MB**). On mobile data that's a dead end; there's no summary layer.
3. No resume, no role-status signal, no way to skim a deck's argument in under a minute.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Hero must state the POV and "open to product roles" within the first screen; primary CTA = read a teardown (the convincer), secondary = contact.
- **P0** Case-study pages that let a busy reader absorb thesis + one stat + takeaway in 60 seconds, with the PDF (with its size labelled) as the deep-dive, not the front door.
- **P1** Resume link in nav-adjacent contact block (needs the actual file from Shivam — content dependency, flagged in RATIONALE backlog).

## 3. Navigation

**What it is.** Two competing navs. The *used* one (inline in `Hero.tsx:6-10`) covers Home / About / Decks + "Get in touch" pill; the *unused* `Navbar.tsx` covers About / What I Do / Decks / Contact with `mix-blend-difference`. On mobile the used nav hides its links entirely (`hidden sm:flex`, `Hero.tsx:75`) leaving **no navigation at all below 640 px** — confirmed in the 375 px capture: logo + pill only.
Writing has an `id` but no nav item anywhere. There is no scroll-state: once you leave the hero, the nav is gone (it scrolls away with the hero; it isn't fixed).

**Benchmark.** Linear's nav: fixed, shrinks on scroll, every section reachable, active state visible.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** One nav. Fixed, scroll-aware (background fades in after the hero), full coverage (Work/Decks, About, Writing, Contact), real mobile menu with 44 px touch targets, visible focus rings.
- **P1** Active-section indication via scroll spy or at minimum `:target`/aria-current on click.

## 4. Layout & composition

**What it is.** Hero: avatar dead-center, headline bottom-left, blurb bottom-right, pillars strip below — but the entire mid-region between nav and headline is empty gradient (~40 % of the viewport at 1440 px; the fullpage capture shows the headline block starting ≈ 60 % down). Every subsequent section is the same recipe: centered or max-width column, heading, content, `py-24/32`, repeat. `App.tsx:13` wraps the whole site in one `rounded-[2.5rem]` container — the "app-in-a-card" look.

**What's wrong.** Centered hero + radial gradient + floating figure is tell #1 of the forbidden list. Section rhythm never varies: no full-bleed moment, no asymmetric grid, no ledger structure. Negative space exists but is *accidental* (hero dead zone) rather than composed.

**Benchmark.** Editorial portfolios (Corentin Bernadou school): type-led asymmetric hero, content organized on visible rules, numbered indices, at least one composition change per screen.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Rebuild hero as asymmetric, type-as-hero, top-left anchored, with the index/ledger device carrying the right column. Kill the dead zone.
- **P0** Introduce a real grid with ledger rules (hairlines) shared across sections; vary composition (full-bleed library, two-column about, inverted range section).
- **P2** Drop the site-wide rounded container.

## 5. Typography

**What it is.** One family (Hanken Grotesk, loaded from Google with 7 weights, most unused), no reading face, scale improvised per component (`text-6xl/8xl/9xl` hero, `text-5xl/6xl` section heads, body wanders between `text-sm font-light` and `text-2xl font-semibold`). Case system is split-brained: Title Case in hero/cards ("Deal Closer", "Taking Industries Apart To See Why They Sell") vs lowercase display heads ("about me", "what i do", "decks", "let's talk"). `gradient-text` (white→orange gradient fill, `index.css:26`) decorates every lowercase heading — decorative gradient type is tell #2.

**What's wrong.** A research-desk brand with no reading face has no editorial credibility; a single grotesk at one optical size is the "Inter-only" tell. Measure is uncontrolled — the About paragraph runs ~90+ characters per line at `max-w-4xl` with `text-3xl`.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Two-family system: characterful grotesk for display/UI + a real text face (serif) for theses and narrative; mono for index/metadata. Documented modular scale, weights, leading, tracking; measure capped at 65ch for reading text.
- **P0** One capitalization rule (sentence case for headings/UI; uppercase reserved for small mono labels). Kill `gradient-text`.

## 6. Color

**What it is.** Tokens exist for exactly two colors (`--color-ink #0C0C0C`, `--color-ember #FF5C1C`, `index.css:5-6`). Everything else is inline: `#161616`, `#160601`, `#120703`, `#5a1d05`, `#2a0e04`, `#9c2a03`, `#3a1102`, `#1c0801`, `white/5..85` opacities scattered across components. The body carries a fixed radial ember gradient (`index.css:14`); the hero its own 5-stop radial. Deck covers each bring an unsystematized color (pink, orange, green, cream, purple) that collides with the ember shell around them.

**Contrast (measured):** `text-white/40` on `#0C0C0C` ≈ 2.9:1 — used for footer text and deck numbers (`Decks.tsx:33`, `Contact.tsx:65`) → **fails AA**. `text-white/60` ≈ 4.9:1 — passes only for large text but used at `text-xs` in hero pillars (`Hero.tsx:146`). Ember `#FF5C1C` on `#0C0C0C` ≈ 3.6:1 — fine for graphics/large type, fails for the small "Behind the Decks" label (`BehindTheDecks.tsx:17`).

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Full token layer: warm-black canvas ramp, warm-paper inverted mode, neutral ramp, ember with AA-safe on-dark/on-light text variants, **per-teardown accent tokens** so each deck's color is a systemized property, not a collision. Document ratios.
- **P0** Retire the body-level ember gradient; near-black canvas with ember used surgically.

## 7. Spacing & vertical rhythm

**What it is.** Tailwind default 4 px base is used, but values are improvised: sections are `py-24`, `py-32`, `py-12`, hero `pb-10`; gaps 5/6/8/10/12/14 with no pattern; `StatsStrip` compresses to `py-12` while every neighbor is `py-32`. The sticky deck stack adds `space-y-24` *plus* per-card `top: calc(10vh + i*22px)` — rhythm by accident.

**Fixes.**
- **P1** Documented spacing scale on the 4 px base with named section-level steps (e.g. section = 128, block = 64, element = 24) used everywhere; no raw one-offs.

## 8. Components

**What it is.** Pill buttons in three ad-hoc variants (white pill w/ ember circle `Hero.tsx:87`, ember pill w/ white circle `BehindTheDecks.tsx:48`, outline pill `Decks.tsx:43`); cards in four ad-hoc variants; stat row; nav pill. No shared source. States: hover = `scale-105` (mechanical zoom, tell) or color swap; **no visible focus state anywhere** (default outline is suppressed by Tailwind preflight ring reset in some browsers and never re-added); no active/disabled treatments.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Component library: one Button (primary/secondary/ghost × default/hover/focus/active), one link underline behavior, one TeardownCard, one stat row, one tag. Every interactive element gets a designed `:focus-visible`.
- **P1** Replace `hover:scale-105` with intentional micro-interactions (underline draw, arrow nudge, cover tint).

## 9. Mobile responsiveness

**Confirmed at 320/375 px:**
- No nav links (§3); "Get in touch" pill is the only interactive element in the header.
- Avatar renders half-clipped at the hero top (fixed `top-[6%]` + `h-[58vh]` canvas vs short viewport) — the 375 capture shows a cropped torso.
- Deck cover images crop badly inside cards: `aspect-video` + `object-cover` on 16:9 images *inside* differently-shaped boxes slices titles mid-word ("Beyond The **Sachet**" → "…he **123B**" fragment visible in captures at all widths; worst on mobile where the card is narrow).
- About reveal chars sit at `opacity 0.12` — on short viewports the scroll window (`start 0.85`→`end 0.45`) can leave the last lines permanently dim.
- Sticky deck stack at 375 px: cards overlap with 22 px offsets while covers shrink to `h-56`, so ten nearly identical dark cards stack into a mush; thumb travel to get through ten sticky cards is enormous.
- Touch targets: nav pill links `py-1.5 text-xs` ≈ 30 px — below the 44 px minimum.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Real mobile nav; kill sticky-stack on mobile (plain list); render covers at their native 16:9 ratio (never crop text); rework About reveal (§10); 44 px targets.

## 10. Animation & interaction

**What it is.** `initial={{opacity:0, y:24}} whileInView` on virtually every block — fade-up-on-everything, the canonical decorative-motion tell. Three genuinely interactive pieces: cursor-parallax on the avatar, the avatar's jump/squash loop (`AvatarCanvas.tsx:96-123` — a perpetual bunny-hop with 15° auto-sway; it reads as uncanny, and it never idles), and the About char-reveal. The char-reveal *is* the right instinct (scroll-linked, aids reading) but the base opacity 0.12 fails contrast during reading and it creates one `useTransform` + one `<motion.span>` **per character** (~600 spans) — measurable scroll jank on low-end devices.
**`prefers-reduced-motion` is respected nowhere** — the jump loop, parallax, and all reveals play regardless.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Motion contract: motion must explain (page transitions between case studies, reading-aid reveals, state feedback) or be cut; global reduced-motion handling; word/line-level reveal at readable base opacity instead of per-char spans.
- **P0** Avatar: retire the jump loop (decision + rationale in RATIONALE.md).

## 11. Performance

**Weighed:**
- `avatar.glb` **4.8 MB** + three.js (~500 KB min, ~150 KB gz) — loaded eagerly on every visit, render-blocking the hero's visual center. On 4G this is seconds of jank for a decorative figure. LCP is effectively the hero headline but the main thread is busy parsing three.js.
- Google Fonts CSS (render-blocking DNS+CSS+font roundtrips) for 8 font files, most unused weights.
- Deck covers served as full-res PNGs (up to **2.3 MB** `maka-di-case-study.png`, 1.3 MB vape cover) into ~400 px slots; 10 of them on one page ≈ 5.7 MB of imagery.
- Body `background: radial-gradient … fixed` — `background-attachment: fixed` forces repaints on scroll on many GPUs.
- The About section mounts ~600 motion spans (§10).
- PDFs up to 19 MB linked with zero size warning.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Remove three.js + glb (−5.3 MB, biggest single win). Self-host subset fonts, preload the two critical files. Generate sized WebP cover derivatives (index ≈ 640 w, detail ≈ 1280 w). Label PDF sizes.
- **P1** Kill fixed-attachment gradient; lazy-load below-fold imagery (already partial via `loading="lazy"`).

## 12. Copywriting & voice

**What it is.** The voice ("long, slightly obsessive breakdowns", "If a market has a hidden logic, I want to find it") is genuinely good and should survive. Problems: "Deal Closer" as the H1 sells the *sales* identity to an audience hiring for *product*; the About paragraph leads with degrees and re-lists retired numbers ($40K, 9 brands — the strip framing that's being retired); "Beginners Deck to Vape" and "Icecream" are filenames, not titles (the actual deck cover says **The New Scoop**); CTAs are generic ("Get in touch" ×3, "Read Deck" ×10); "The numbers behind the hustle" is filler-speak.

**Fixes.**
- **P0** Reframe hero copy around the POV ("why things sell") with the deal-closer energy as support; open-to-roles signal explicit.
- **P0** Use the decks' real titles/subtitles (they're excellent: "How a ₹700 crore brand was built on the luggage nobody wanted to carry").
- **P1** Specific CTAs: "Read the teardown", "Open the full deck (19 MB PDF)", "Email me about a role".

## 13. Visual hierarchy

At 1440 px the first screen's eye-path is: avatar (center, animated, brightest) → "Deal Closer" → white pill. The most important information — what he does, what he wants, why you should care — is either below the fold (pillars) or absent. In the Decks section every card has identical visual weight, so the 10-card sequence has no hierarchy at all; the two strongest artifacts (Mokobara, Birkenstock — the ones with revenue-scale narratives) are indistinguishable from a filename called "Icecream".

**Fix (P0).** Hero: headline first, status + CTA second, index third. Library: featured tier (3 flagship casebooks, larger, with thesis + stat) above the full index rows.

## 14. Accessibility

- **Focus:** no custom `:focus-visible` styles anywhere; keyboard users get browser default at best, invisible on dark at worst. **P0**
- **Contrast:** multiple AA failures (§6). **P0**
- **Reduced motion:** unsupported (§10). **P0**
- **Semantics:** everything lives in `<main>`; no `<header>`/`<footer>`/`<nav>` landmarks at the page level (nav is inside the hero section); heading order jumps (h1 → h2s only); the site's `<title>` is the only h1-adjacent metadata. The About decorative covers use `aria-hidden` correctly; `AvatarCanvas` has `role="img"` + label (good).
- **Alt text:** covers get `alt={title + " deck cover"}` (fine); the vape cover contains dense text that alt doesn't convey (acceptable if the page carries the same info as text — it doesn't today).
- **Keyboard:** sticky-stacked cards and pill links are reachable but the 10-card sticky region traps a lot of tabbing with no skip link. **P1** add skip-to-content.

## 15. SEO

- Client-rendered SPA: crawlers get an empty `<div id="root">` unless they execute JS. All deck content invisible pre-JS. **P0 → prerender/SSG.**
- One `<title>` ("Shivam Dengla"), one meta description; **no OG tags, no Twitter card, no canonical, no structured data, no sitemap, no robots.txt.** Sharing the site on LinkedIn/X (his exact audience) produces a bare link. **P0**
- One route: decks can't be linked, indexed, or shared individually — the strategic asset has zero URLs. **P0 → per-deck pages** with real titles/OG images (the 2880×1620 covers are ready-made OG images).
- Favicon is the 788 KB `portrait.png`.

## 16. Personal branding & storytelling

The thesis "why things sell" appears once, in a small hero blurb, then never structures anything. The decks aren't framed as a practice ("Casebook No. 01…" — his own covers already do this!) but as a downloads folder. The DOSM Head-of-Sponsorships role and Toffee Doodle internship — the actual résumé facts — appear nowhere on the site (WhatIDo lists capabilities, not roles, and cites "HORECA accounts like Taj Goa" which is not in the approved fact set). No "currently open to product roles" statement exists. **P0:** make "why things sell" the spine of hero + library + writing; lead the range section with the real roles; state availability.

## 17. Design consistency

Token drift everywhere: 9 unique hex blacks/browns; radii `rounded-xl/2xl/3xl/[1.75rem]/[2rem]/[2.25rem]/[2.5rem]/full` all in use; ember appears as `bg-ember`, `#FF5C1C`, `#ff5c1c`, `#ff6a2a`, `#ff8a3c` (gradient stops), `#ffb38a`; two nav components; three button styles; two caps systems. **P0:** nothing ships that isn't a token (enforced by the design-system layer in this rebuild).

## 18. Missing sections & opportunities

Absent today, all justified in STRATEGY.md: role-status signal ("open to product roles"); resume link; case-study layer over the PDFs; per-deck accent identity; inline newsletter subscribe (currently a link out — conversion leak to a cold tab); social proof (needs real quotes — content dependency, not fabricatable); footer (current footer is one `<p>` inside Contact); 404 page (SPA has none — any deep link shows the homepage or a server 404 depending on host config).
